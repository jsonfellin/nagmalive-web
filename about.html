Our story

Built by musicians, for musicians.

The people, the frustration, and the practice sessions that turned into NagmaLive.

NagmaLive was born from a simple need: musicians practicing Indian classical music needed a reliable, high-quality digital companion that could replace the limitations of older electronic tanpura and tabla machines.

What started as a personal project quickly grew into something much bigger. We realized that tabla players and Kathak dancers around the world all shared the same frustration: practicing alone without quality accompaniment.

Our Mission

We're dedicated to preserving and promoting Indian classical music by making practice more accessible, enjoyable, and effective. NagmaLive combines authentic sounds with modern technology to give every musician — from beginner students to seasoned professionals — the tools they need to grow.

What Makes Us Different

  • Real lehra recordings on sarangi, sitar, and harmonium, not synthesized sounds
  • Live tabla theka recordings for Kathak practice
  • Independent pitch and tempo control across Tintal, Jhaptal, Ektal, Rupak, and Dhamar
  • Built by musicians, for musicians
