What's inside

Accompaniment that sounds live, because it was.

Built for tabla players and Kathak dancers who need a lehra that holds the cycle.

01 Melody

Real lehra

38 lehra recordings on sarangi, sitar, and harmonium across Tintal, Jhaptal, Ektal, Rupak, and Dhamar. Change the tempo without changing the pitch, and tune the pitch to match your tabla.

5 taals · 15 raags · Independent pitch & tempo

02 Dance

Kathak mode

Adds live tabla theka recordings under the lehra, matched to its taal and pitch, so dancers practice with the full accompaniment they perform with.

Recorded tabla theka · Matched taal & pitch

03 Atmosphere

Tanpura, alap & more

A tanpura drone tuned automatically to each raag, alap intros for a live feel, gat recordings for practicing accompaniment, and real jhala tracks for drut Tintal.

Tanpura · Alap · Gats · Jhala
